## Fruit Frenzy meter

The meter drains all the time, full to empty in 18 seconds on Normal at the default slider, and an empty meter ends the COMBO. Difficulty scales it: Easy drains at 0.55x with slices worth 1.5x, Hard 1.5x and 0.65x, Insane 2x and 0.5x.

Normal, from the bot in Azahar, which slices one fruit at a time and rarely swipes several. A person's swipe bonuses fill the meter faster.

## Sound delay

Measured from the moment a sound is cued to what Windows sends to the speakers, with a WASAPI loopback capture: Windows about 25 ms, Linux in WSLg about 45 ms (it passes through the remote-desktop audio link). The game now asks for a 256-frame buffer (about 5 ms) and trims silence at the start of sound files: Fruit Frenzy 99 ms, Insane combo 49 ms, Legendary 19 ms, throw 10 ms, swishes 4-7 ms. The 3DS and real phones were not measured.

## Combo sound level

Every combo sound plays at a loudest-tenth-of-a-second RMS of 0.15, the level of the loudest slices. One that lands while another is ringing drops by equal power, never below 60%. Worst-case mix test (big combos and full swipes every 1.2 s, universal mixer): peak 0.90, none over full scale, against 1.20 before.

## Not assessed

- **Sound by ear**, and **stereo 3D** on a console with a 3D screen.
- **The 3D HOME Menu banner.** Azahar has no HOME Menu here, so neither banner was seen on screen. The flat banner ships.
